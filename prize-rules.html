Prize rules

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, Meta, Apple or Google.

1. The prize

For each challenge, the person with the most challenge points at the end of the challenge wins 1 month (30 days) of free access to Health Log, added after any access they already have. Approximate value: US$10. The prize can't be exchanged for cash or transferred.

2. Who can take part

  • Anyone with a paid pass for that challenge who is at least 18 years old (or has a parent or guardian's permission), and lives where this promotion is allowed. Void where prohibited.
  • The organiser and their household can take part for fun but can't win.

3. How points are counted

  • Each day you can earn up to 100 points: up to 20 each for steps, water, sleep, workouts and food, based on your own goals. A completed fast of 16+ hours counts as full food points.
  • Points count from the challenge's start date (or the day your 75-day streak starts) until 11:59 PM on its last day, in your own time zone.
  • The winner is decided by effort and consistency, not by chance.

4. Ties

If two or more people have the same points, the one with more counted challenge days wins. If they are still tied, each of them wins the prize.

5. Checking the winner

  • We'll contact the likely winner by email within 14 days after the challenge ends.
  • We may ask for proof, such as screenshots from Apple Health, Google Fit or a fitness watch, which must be sent within 7 days. If proof isn't sent, or the logs look false, we'll move to the next person.
  • Our decision is final.

6. Other rules

  • We may share the winner's first name and challenge results on our Instagram, unless the winner asks us not to.
  • If something outside our control stops a challenge from running fairly, we may change or cancel the prize for that challenge.
  • Winners are responsible for any taxes on the prize, if any apply.

7. Contact

Reply to your welcome email, or message us on Instagram. Please don't send health details by message.

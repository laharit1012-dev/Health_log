Fill in steps from Apple Health (iPhone)
  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +. Name it Sync steps.
  2. Add Find Health Samples: Type Steps, Start Date is today.
  3. Add Calculate Statistics: Sum of the Health Samples.
  4. Add Round Number (to ones).
  5. Add URL: https://laharit1012-dev.github.io/Health_log/?steps= and then insert the Rounded Number after the = sign.
  6. Add Open URLs. Tap ▶ once and allow Health access.
  7. To make it automatic: Automation tab → + → Time of Day (for example 9 PM, daily) → Run Immediately → choose Sync steps.

It opens Health Log in Safari, so stay signed in there too. If you wear an Apple Watch, add the filter Source is your iPhone in step 2 so steps aren't counted twice.

    Badges

      How points, levels and daily titles work

      Points each day: up to 100, shared across five logs.

      • Steps: up to 20, your share of your step goal
      • Water: up to 20, your share of your glasses goal
      • Sleep: up to 20, your share of your sleep goal
      • Workout: up to 20, your share of your workout minutes goal
      • Food: up to 20, for logging 3 meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack)

      Daily title: 1+ points Warming up · 50+ Go-getter · 75+ Achiever · 100 Superstar.

      Levels: a day with 50+ points is a consistent day. Get 5 consistent days in a week (Monday to Sunday) to move up a level. Levels never go down.

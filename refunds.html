Refund policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

We want you to enjoy your challenge. If it isn't right for you, here's how refunds work.

Full refund

  • Within 3 days of paying, for any reason; or
  • Any time before your challenge starts, if you bought it early.

If something doesn't work

If the app doesn't work on your phone and we can't fix it within a few days, we'll refund you in full, whenever you paid.

No refund

  • After the first 3 days of a challenge that has started, for example for missing days or changing your mind.
  • Free access, including prizes.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the terms, such as sharing an account or false logs.

How to ask

Reply to your welcome email with the email you paid with. Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days, and your challenge access ends when the refund is made.

Please ask us before disputing a charge with your bank. We're happy to help, and it's usually much faster.
